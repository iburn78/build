# Model structure

Last edited: 2026-10-21

## Profile

- **Purpose:** Represents a listed company or tracked company business.
- **Lookup:** `Profile.get_item(key)`; `key` identifies the company record and `code` stores its market code.
- **Persisted information:** `Overview`, `Business` (`InfoSection`), `News`, and generated `financials`.
- **Refresh:** Overview and news refresh on their configured schedules. Unreviewed business information can be regenerated from the overview.
- **Financial end key:** The profile's own company key.

## Segment

- **Purpose:** Represents a virtual segment of a company.
- **Lookup:** `Segment.get_item(key)`; segment keys commonly include a suffix such as `005930(A)`.
- **Relationship:** Uses its parent profile's financial data and applies a `FinancialsAdjuster` for revenue, operating margin, and market-cap shares.
- **Financial end key:** The parent profile key with the segment identifier.

## Component

- **Purpose:** Groups listed companies or company segments that participate in an industry component.
- **Lookup:** `Component.get_item(key, namelist=[...])` or `Component.get_item(key, keylist=[...])`.
- **Persisted relationship:** `listed_companies` contains `ListedCompany` records with a company/segment `key` and display `name`.
- **Other information:** `Traits` (`InfoSection`), `News`, and generated `financials`.
- **Creation requirement:** A new component needs at least one listed company.
- **Financial end keys:** The keys in `listed_companies`.

## ValueChain

- **Purpose:** Orders related components into an industry value chain.
- **Lookup:** `ValueChain.get_item(key, component_keys=[...])` or `ValueChain.get_item(key, components=[...])`.
- **Inputs:** `component_keys` accepts component names/keys; `components` accepts `Component` instances. Supply one form. `components` is an input convenience; JSON persists the references as `component_keys`.
- **Validation:** Before loading sub-items, the value chain checks that each referenced component JSON exists and validates. Missing or invalid references raise a `ValueError` that identifies the components.
- **Other information:** `Landscape` (`InfoSection`), `News`, and generated `financials`.
- **Financial end keys:** The union of listed-company keys across its components.

## Shared model conventions

- `InfoSection` stores reviewed qualitative information, tags, notes, and search themes.
- `financials` is generated by `SectorAnalysis`; it is not hand-maintained source information.
- Relationships are persisted by keys. `_sub_items` is an in-memory map populated while models are loaded or refreshed.
- Components and value chains support industry, competitive, and economic context in their `Traits` and `Landscape` sections.

## Value-chain analysis topics

- Supply relationships and critical components
- Relative economics within each component and across the chain
- Demand drivers for key components and final products
- Sources and direction of structural change

Typical structures include a materials-to-manufacturing chain (raw materials → materials suppliers → manufacturers → consumers) and a capital-expenditure chain (infrastructure and equipment suppliers → manufacturers, with maintenance and outsourced services).
